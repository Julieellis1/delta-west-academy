Why Delta West

We build an “I can do that” attitude.

Psychologist Dr. Albert Bandura calls it self-efficacy: a person's belief in their ability to succeed. At DWA, small classes give teachers time with every student, for mastery experiences, honest feedback and the confidence to take on hard things.

Students with strong self-efficacy view challenging problems as tasks to be mastered, recover quickly from setbacks, and are less vulnerable to stress and anxiety.

“I can do that: for myself, my future, my world.”

Since 1993

A different vision for education.

Delta West Academy opened in 1993 with 11 students in a downtown office building, built on a simple premise: small classes and real relationships.

The name says it. Our mission is to make a difference and create change in the lives of our students.

Δ deltameans “the difference in”

  1. 1993

    Society founded and provincially accredited; opened with 11 students.

  2. 1998

    Moved to the historic Bridgeland School, adding a playing field and gym.

  3. 1999

    Kindergarten added.

  4. 2004

    Purchased the Bridgeland School as its permanent home.

  5. 2026

    Full-day JK & K programming launched.
